## To do

What a privacy policy typically covers (the exact duty depends on your country
and on what the site does):

- who is responsible for the data processing, and how to contact them
- what your hosting provider logs (such as IP addresses) and for how long
- every plugin, form, font, embed or analytics tool that processes visitor
  data, its purpose and legal basis
- how long data is kept
- the visitor's rights (access, correction, deletion, objection) and where to
  complain
